This guide walks you through your first edit in Daydream, from setup to a finished video.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect an AI agent">
    Daydream is built to edit with AI, so start by connecting an agent. Follow [Connect Claude Code or Codex](/guides/connect-claude-code-codex) to set it up.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import your assets">
    In the **Assets** tab, click **Import**, then add your files based on how they'll be used:

    - **Voiceover / Talking Head**: your main narration or on-camera footage. Daydream transcribes these for your script.
    - **Footage / Images / Music**: cutaways, b-roll, images, and audio. The AI watches your video clips so it can use them in your edit.

    Then click **Import**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/import-assets.png" alt="Daydream Import Assets dialog with Voiceover / Talking Head and Footage / Images / Music categories" style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }} />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Transcribe your voiceover">
    Go to the **Editor** tab and click **Transcribe Video** or **Transcribe Voiceover**. Daydream transcribes your voiceover and adds it to the timeline, so you can edit your video by editing its transcript.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/transcribe.png" alt="Daydream Editor tab with Transcribe Video and Transcribe Voiceover buttons" style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }} />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>
