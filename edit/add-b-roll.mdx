To add B-roll in Daydream, describe the shot you want and where it goes, like "put the shot of the coffee being poured over the part where I talk about my morning". The AI has already watched your footage, so it finds the right moment in the right clip and places it over your talking head.

## Get your footage ready

Import B-roll under **Footage / Images / Music** (see [import your footage](/edit/import-footage)). The AI watches each clip in the background and writes down what's in it, so later it can find a shot from a description without going through everything again.

You can start asking for B-roll as soon as a clip finishes analyzing. Images work too, and so do clips you imported under **Voiceover / Talking Head**. The AI looks at those on the spot instead.

## Ask for B-roll

> Add B-roll over the part where I talk about the beach.

> Put the shot of the dog running at 0:15, for about 3 seconds.

> Cover every sentence in the first 30 seconds with a different shot. Don't repeat any clips.

> Use only clips from the Tokyo trip for this section.

The AI places B-roll on the track above your main footage (**V2**), lined up with the words it covers. The B-roll's own sound comes along on the matching audio track. If you don't want it, ask the AI to mute it.

If you don't like a pick, say what's wrong: "that shot is too dark, find another one" or "use the wider shot of the kitchen instead".

## Add B-roll by hand

- Select words in the **Transcript** panel, click the B-roll button that appears next to them, and pick a clip. It's placed over exactly those words on **V2**.
- Or drag a clip from **Assets** onto a track above your main footage.

Trim it, move it or swap it like any other clip. See [edit on the timeline](/edit/timeline).

## Tips for better picks

- Describe what's in the shot, not the file name. "The close-up of hands typing" beats "clip_0042".
- Say how long. B-roll that runs over a whole paragraph feels slow. A shot per sentence usually feels right for short-form.
- Mix in a style request. "Add B-roll and a slow zoom on each shot" saves a second round.

## Limits

- Daydream analyzes videos up to 60 minutes long. Trim longer videos before importing them as B-roll. The free plan includes 1 hour of footage analysis a month, and paid plans include more. See [plans and limits](/help/plans-and-limits).
- The AI can't generate new footage or find stock footage online. It works with what you import.
