Everything the AI does in Daydream, you can also do by hand on the timeline. Drag clips to move them, drag their edges to trim, press **Cmd+B** (**Ctrl+B** on Windows) to split, and use **Properties** for position, scale and crop.

## Move, trim and split

- **Move** a clip by dragging it. It snaps to nearby clip edges and the playhead. Hold **Option** (**Alt** on Windows) while dragging to turn snapping off for that move, or turn it off with the magnet button in the toolbar.
- **Trim** by dragging either end of a clip.
- **Split** with **Cmd+B** (**Ctrl+B**). If one clip is selected, it splits that clip at the playhead. Otherwise it splits every clip under the playhead. You can also switch to the scissors tool in the toolbar and click a clip where you want to cut it.
- **Delete** a clip with **Delete** or **Backspace**. This leaves a gap. Cuts made in the transcript close the gap for you, but deleting on the timeline doesn't.

Select several clips by **Cmd**-clicking (**Ctrl**-clicking) them, or drag a box around them on an empty part of the timeline. **Cmd+A** (**Ctrl+A**) selects every clip.

**Cmd+C** and **Cmd+V** (**Ctrl+C** and **Ctrl+V**) copy and paste clips, including into another open project.

## Change a clip's speed

Right-click a video or audio clip and pick a speed: 0.25x, 0.5x, 1x, 1.5x, 2x or 4x. Choose **Custom...** for anything from 0.25x to 20x. The clip gets shorter or longer to match, and voices keep their normal pitch.

Speed applies to the whole clip. To speed up only part of one, split it first. Or ask the AI: "speed up the part where I'm walking to the car."

## Position, scale, rotate and crop

Select a clip and open **Properties**:

- **Transform**: position, scale, rotation and opacity
- **Crop**: trim the top, bottom, left or right edge

You can also drag the clip in the preview to move it, and drag its handles to resize or rotate it. Rotation snaps every 15 degrees. Hold **Shift** to rotate freely.

To animate any of these over time, like a slow zoom, use the diamond keyframe buttons next to each value. [Add zooms and camera moves](/style/zoom-effects) explains keyframes.

## Tracks

Video tracks are numbered **V1**, **V2** and up, and audio tracks **A1**, **A2** and up. Higher video tracks sit on top of lower ones, so B-roll on V2 covers your main footage on V1. Captions have their own **CC** track.

- Add or remove a track pair with the track buttons in the toolbar. You can have up to 8 video and 8 audio tracks.
- The eye button on a track hides it, or mutes it for audio tracks.
- Drag the line between tracks to make one taller.

## Linked clips

When you add a video with sound, its picture and audio are linked: they move, trim, split and delete together. Turn **Link Clips** off in the toolbar to move them separately. To link other clips together, like a screen recording and a face cam, see [sync audio and cameras](/edit/sync-audio).

## Undo

**Cmd+Z** undoes, and **Cmd+Shift+Z** redoes (**Ctrl+Z** and **Ctrl+Shift+Z** on Windows). Each open project keeps its own history of up to 50 steps.

## Zoom and scroll

- **Cmd** (**Ctrl**) plus scrolling zooms the timeline. So do the zoom buttons.
- **Shift+Z** fits the whole edit in view.
- When there are more tracks than fit, scrolling moves up and down through them. Otherwise it moves left and right. **Shift** plus scrolling always moves left and right.
