To sync a separate mic or a second camera with your video in Daydream, put both on the timeline and ask the AI to sync them. It lines them up by matching their audio, the same way editors sync by waveform, and can link them so later cuts keep them together.

## Sync a mic or second camera

1. Import both recordings and put them on the timeline. Rough positions are fine.
2. Ask:

> Sync my lav recording to the camera and link them.

> Line up the B camera with the A camera.

The clip you name as the main one stays where it is, and the others move to match it. Linking means a cut, trim or transcript edit later applies to all of them, so they never drift apart. Measuring takes a few seconds for short clips and a few minutes for long 4K files.

Once they're synced, you'll usually want only one audio source. Ask the AI to "mute the camera audio", or click the eye button on that audio track, which hides everything on that track.

## Picture-in-picture: screen recording and face cam

For a tutorial or a reaction video, you can put a small face cam over a full-screen recording:

> Put the face cam in the bottom right corner over the screen recording, sync it by audio, and link them.

The AI syncs the two recordings, shrinks the face cam, positions it, and links everything. Because they're linked, cutting silences from your narration removes the same moment from the screen recording too.

To give the screen recording a rounded, shadowed window look, ask for the window frame effect. See [add effects](/style/effects).

## When syncing doesn't work

Syncing needs both recordings to have picked up the same sound. It won't work when:

- one recording is silent, like a screen recording without audio
- the recordings were made in different rooms
- a clip's speed has been changed from 1x
- the clips don't overlap in time at all

In those cases, the AI tells you it couldn't find a match rather than guessing. Give it something to line up instead, like a clap at the start ("sync them on the clap at the beginning") or a phrase both recordings caught.

You can also line clips up by hand: drag one until the waveforms match, then ask the AI to link them.

On long recordings, the AI may warn about drift, which means the two devices' clocks ran at slightly different speeds. The sync is most accurate in the middle, so check the start and end by ear.
