To export a video from Daydream, click **Export**, choose **Video**, pick where it's going (**YouTube**, **TikTok/Reels** or **Web**), and click **Export to file**. Each preset picks a resolution and quality for you. You can change the resolution on any preset, and **Custom** also lets you set the quality.

You can also ask the AI: "export this for TikTok and save it to my Desktop."

## Which preset should I use?

| Preset | Use it for | Resolution |
|---|---|---|
| **YouTube** | Long-form videos on YouTube | Up to 4K, matching your footage |
| **TikTok/Reels** | Vertical short-form videos on TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts | 720p or 1080p |
| **Web** | A smaller file for Slack, email or a website | 720p or 1080p, smaller file |
| **Custom** | Choosing everything yourself | 720p, 1080p or 4K |

### Best export settings for YouTube

Use **YouTube**. It exports at the highest resolution your footage supports, up to 4K. If you shot in 4K, you get a 4K file.

### Best export settings for TikTok, Reels and Shorts

Use **TikTok/Reels**. These apps play video at 1080p, so a larger file wouldn't look any sharper once uploaded. Make sure the project is vertical first. See [make a vertical video](/vertical-video).

### Best export settings for sharing a link or file

Use **Web** for a small file that uploads and sends quickly.

## Resolution and quality

- **Resolution**: 720p and 1080p on every preset, plus 4K on **YouTube** and **Custom**. The highest resolution in your timeline is marked **Source**.
- **Quality** (**Custom** only): **Low** (smallest file), **Medium** (the default) or **High** (largest file, best quality).

If most of your footage is lower resolution than you picked, Daydream warns you, because scaling it up makes the file bigger without adding detail. Hover the info icon next to the warning to see which ones.

## Frame rate

**Frame rate** is set to **Auto** unless you change it. Auto uses the frame rate of most of your footage. It goes higher when some of your footage runs at double that rate or more, and up to 60 fps when the video has captions or animated graphics, so they move smoothly. You can also choose 23.976, 24, 25, 29.97, 30, 50, 59.94 or 60.

The frame rate is saved with the project and used for the preview and every export.

## What you get

An MP4 file (H.264 video, AAC audio) that plays everywhere. Daydream asks where to save it and remembers that folder for next time. When it's done, you'll see **Export complete**. Click the file name to show it in Finder or File Explorer.

Daydream uses your computer's video encoder when it can, which is fast. If that isn't available, it falls back to a slower one, so the same export can take 3 to 4 times longer.

## While it exports

The export window shows each step and the progress. You can cancel at any time. Only one export runs at a time, across all your open projects.

## Limits

- Exports can be up to 2 hours long.
- Daydream exports MP4 only. Audio-only files, GIFs and other formats aren't available yet.
- Daydream checks there's enough disk space before it starts.

If an export fails, see [troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting#export-problems).
