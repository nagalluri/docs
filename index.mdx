Daydream is a desktop video editor for Mac and Windows that you edit with AI. You describe the change you want in chat, Claude or ChatGPT makes it on a real timeline, and you watch it happen. If you don't like something, you undo it, ask again, or fix it by hand.

All of your footage stays local on your device and is never uploaded or stored in the cloud.

## What can you do with it?

Most people use Daydream for the slow parts of editing:

- Cut silences, filler words and bad takes by reading the transcript instead of scrubbing the timeline.
- Add B-roll by describing it ("put the shot of the coffee being poured here"), because the AI has already watched your footage.
- Add captions, titles, lower thirds and animated graphics.
- Zoom in, color grade, blur your background, put text behind you, and cut a montage to the beat of a song.
- Export an MP4, or send the edit to Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, Final Cut Pro or CapCut to finish it there.

Everything the AI does lands on the timeline as normal clips, keyframes and settings, so you can always see what it changed and adjust it yourself.

## What do you need?

- A Mac (Apple silicon, or an Intel Mac from 2016 or later, on macOS 13 or later) or a Windows 10 or 11 PC.
- A Claude or ChatGPT subscription. The AI runs on your own account, and Daydream sets up the connection for you.
- A Daydream account. The free plan includes the full editor and unlimited exports with no watermark. See [plans and limits](/help/plans-and-limits).
