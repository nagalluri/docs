Daydream has three plans: Free, Creator and Pro. Every plan includes the full editor and unlimited exports with no watermark. Paid plans add more AI usage, more transcription and footage analysis, and saved styles and recipes.

## Compare plans

| | Free | Creator | Pro |
|---|---|---|---|
| Price | $0 | $25 a month, or $20 a month billed yearly | $50 a month, or $40 a month billed yearly |
| Full editor, exports up to 4K, no watermark | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Export to Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, Final Cut Pro and CapCut | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| AI tool calls | 100 a month | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Transcription | 1 hour a month | 10 hours a month | 30 hours a month |
| Footage analysis for B-roll | 1 hour a month | 20 hours a month | 60 hours a month |
| Saved caption styles and graphics | Not included | 3 of each | Unlimited |
| Recipes | Not included | 1 | Unlimited |
| Priority support and early access | No | No | Yes |

## What counts as a tool call?

Every action the AI takes in your project counts as one tool call: adding a clip, reading the transcript, checking a frame, setting a color grade. A single request like "cut the silences and add captions" usually takes several. Tool calls from [other AI tools connected over MCP](/ai/connect-other-ai-tools) count too.

On the free plan, when you reach 100 in a month, the AI tells you in chat and links to upgrade. You can keep editing by hand until the limit resets.

## Transcription and footage analysis

- **Transcription** counts the length of the audio you transcribe. Transcribing the same file twice doesn't count twice.
- **Footage analysis** counts the length of the video Daydream watches for B-roll search. Each video counts up to its first 60 minutes.

If you reach either limit, Daydream tells you when you try to transcribe or import, and the rest of the editor keeps working.

## Your Daydream plan vs your Claude or ChatGPT plan

These are two separate accounts with separate limits:

- Your **Daydream plan** covers the editor, tool calls, transcription and footage analysis.
- Your **Claude or ChatGPT plan** covers the AI's thinking. Editing in Daydream uses your Claude or ChatGPT plan's usage the same way chatting with Claude or ChatGPT does.

So there are two different limit messages. If chat says **You've hit the usage limit on your Claude plan** (or ChatGPT plan), that's your Claude or ChatGPT plan, and it resets on its own schedule. Switching to a faster model, like Haiku 5.5 or GPT-6 Luna, uses less of it. If the message mentions your monthly tool calls, that's Daydream.

## Upgrade, change or cancel

Click **Upgrade Plan** in Daydream, or go to [daydreamvideo.com/app/manage](https://www.daydreamvideo.com/app/manage). Changing or cancelling your plan happens on the same page.
